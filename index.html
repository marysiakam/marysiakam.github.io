I'm a Senior Product Manager on the Azure Compute team at Microsoft, where I drive OS security and fleet efficiency initiatives across one of the largest clouds in the world. My work sits at the intersection of platform security, fleet efficiency, and reliability-- keeping the systems running Azure's compute fleet safe and performant at hyperscale. Before Azure, I worked on the Microsoft Defender for Endpoint team, where I built cybersecurity operator products used by enterprise and government customers to detect and respond to real world threats. I studied Computer Science and Economics at McGill University in Montreal, a combination that shaped how I think about both the technical tradeoffs and the incentives behind the products I build. Outside of work, I spend most of my time outdoors: rock climbing, trail running, and skiing. When I'm not outside, I read, crochet, and grow vegetables in my garden.
